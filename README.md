*Figure 2: Risks, Checks, and Mitigations associated with Statbarn-Frequencies*

We have colour-coded it to make it more human-readable:
* The statbarn (type of query) is in yellow
* Potential risks are in orange
* Mitigations are in green
* Associated with each risk is a Risk Check (purple) with associated parameters (blue).
* The risk likelihood (beige) is calculated through a combination of (i) check results and (ii) whether a mitigation has been applied. 
* Human Involvement (violet)
